About

BIMstruct is a remote-based structural BIM services company delivering high-quality, practical and affordable BIM solutions — from Cape Town to the world.

We support engineers, contractors, developers, architects and students with reliable, construction‑focused structural BIM services.

Professional BIM Services

Structural Modelling & BIM Management

  • Structural Revit modelling (reinforced concrete & steel)
  • Construction‑ready structural BIM models
  • Model audits, clean‑ups and optimisation
  • BIM execution and model management support

Reinforcement Detailing

  • Rebar modelling, detailing and scheduling
  • Clear, constructible reinforcement layouts
  • Coordination‑ready reinforcement models

BIM Coordination & Clash Detection

  • Multidisciplinary coordination
  • Clash detection and issue tracking
  • Construction‑stage coordination support

3D Visualisation & Renders

  • High‑quality structural visualisation
  • Sequencing and phasing renders
  • Presentation‑ready illustrations

Automation & Dynamo

  • Custom Dynamo script development
  • Revit workflow automation
  • Efficiency tools for BIM teams

Conversions & Digitalisation

  • PDF & CAD to Revit conversion
  • As‑built and record model creation
  • Legacy drawings upgraded to BIM
